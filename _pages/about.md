# About Me

I am an undergraduate student in **Data Science** at **Fudan University**, interested in understanding how the brain works through physical principles, theoretical modeling, and data science. I am also interested in neural coding and brain–computer interfaces, especially in discovering more robust and generalizable patterns of neural representation. I believe such understanding may contribute both to neuroscience and to the development of more intelligent artificial systems. I always enjoy exchanging ideas and would love to connect with researchers in neuroscience, data science, artificial intelligence, medicine, and related fields. (Afternoon tea is on me)

My academic background is interdisciplinary. Before switching my major to Data Science, I studied clinical medicine at **Shanghai Medical College of Fudan University** for two years (2024–2026). During that time, I also joined Shu Lab (@Prof. Shu Yousheng at ITBR) to study neurobiology. My passion for scientific research was sparked during my years at **Shenzhen Middle School** (2018–2024). I was surrounded by many bright and curious minds who thought independently, discussed ideas intensely, and created an atmosphere that I truly enjoyed.
