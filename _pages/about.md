# About Me [CV](/files/resume-zh_EN.pdf)

I am an undergraduate student in **Data Science** at **Fudan University**, interested in understanding how the brain works through physical principles, theoretical modeling, and data-driven methods. I am also interested in neural coding and brain–computer interfaces, especially in discovering more robust and generalizable patterns of neural representation.

My academic background is interdisciplinary. Before switching my major to Data Science, I studied clinical medicine at **Shanghai Medical College of Fudan University** for two years (2024–2026). During that time, I also joined [Shu Lab at ITBR](https://youshenglab.fudan.edu.cn/) to study neurobiology. My passion for scientific research was sparked during my years at **Shenzhen Middle School** (2018–2024). I was surrounded by many bright and curious minds who thought independently, discussed ideas intensely, and created an atmosphere that I truly enjoyed.

As an undergrad, I'm still figuring things out, and I'd be thrilled to connect with anyone fascinated by how the brain works — and how to figure it out. Whether you're from neuroscience, AI, medicine, or just curious, feel free to reach out: [naonaozhong16@gmail.com](mailto:naonaozhong16@gmail.com)

# Research Direction

I am still at an early stage of research training, so I care most about building the right questions and the right toolkit. At present, I am especially drawn to problems where neuroscience, data science, and mathematical modeling meet.

- **Neural representation and coding**: how information is represented, transformed, and stabilized in neural activity.
- **Brain-computer interfaces**: how to discover neural patterns that are robust, interpretable, and generalizable.
- **Mathematical structure of neural data**: how tools from statistics, optimization, and geometry can help characterize high-dimensional neural activity.

I wrote a separate page for my current [research philosophy](/research/), which is more about how I think than what I have already done.
